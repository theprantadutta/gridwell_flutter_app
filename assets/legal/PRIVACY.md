# Privacy Policy for Gridwell: Nonogram Picross

**Legal Version: 1.0**  
**Policy Version: 1.1**  
**Effective Date: November 3, 2026**  
**Last Updated: November 3, 2026**

Gridwell ("we," "our," or "us") is a puzzle game made by Pranta Dutta, an independent developer based in Bangladesh. This policy explains what Gridwell collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

The short version: Gridwell works completely offline and never asks for your name or location. When you are online, it uses an anonymous ID to sync your progress and keep daily leaderboards, and it uses Google services for analytics, crash reports and ads. Signing in with Google or an email address is optional; it only keeps your progress safe across devices.

## 1. What we collect

### Information stored only on your device

Your solved puzzles, best times, boards in progress, daily results, settings, and the puzzles themselves are stored on your device. The game is fully playable without ever sending any of it anywhere.

### Information sent to our server (when you are online)

- **Anonymous ID.** On first use, Gridwell signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a random identifier. Unless you choose to sign in (below), it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account.
- **Account (optional).** If you choose to sign in to keep your progress, we receive your email address and which sign-in you used (Google or email and password). With Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile with Firebase; we store only the email address. With email and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and never reaches our server. Your progress so far moves into the account.
- **Pen name.** A generated name such as "Quiet Heron 214" that appears on daily leaderboards. You can change it in Settings. Please don't use your real name.
- **Puzzle progress.** Which puzzles you have solved, your best times, and when you last played, so progress can be restored and kept in step across reinstalls.
- **Daily puzzle times.** Your time on each daily puzzle, for the leaderboard.
- **Settings.** Your in-app preferences, such as theme and sound.
- **Achievements.** Which ones you have earned.
- **Timezone offset.** The number of minutes between your local time and UTC (for example, +360). It is used to decide which day's daily puzzle is yours and to time reminders. It is not your location.
- **Notification token.** If you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send an occasional message.
- **Purchase records.** If you buy something, the store's receipt or transaction token, which we verify with Google Play or the Apple App Store, plus the resulting entitlement (Remove Ads or Premium and its expiry).

### Information collected by Google services in the app

- **Analytics (Firebase Analytics).** Anonymous events about how the game is played, for example a puzzle started, completed or left unfinished, a hint used, or a setting changed, with details like puzzle size and time taken. Firebase also collects standard device information (model, OS version, app version, language and an app-instance ID) and, on Android, may collect the advertising ID. You can turn analytics off in **Settings > Privacy**.
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** When the app crashes or hits an error: the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and an installation ID.
- **Advertising (Google AdMob).** To show ads in the free version, AdMob processes your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information. With your consent (where consent is required), ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. We collect your email address only if you choose to sign in. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the game's online features: restoring progress (including on a new device when you sign in), the daily leaderboard, and checking purchases.
- To send account emails you ask for, such as confirming your address or resetting your password. We do not send marketing email.
- To understand which puzzles are too hard or too easy and to fix problems (analytics and crash reports).
- To show ads that keep the game free, and to respect Remove Ads and Premium purchases.
- To send an optional reminder or an occasional message, only if you allow notifications. The daily reminder at 7 PM is scheduled on your device and sends nothing to us.
- To prevent abuse of leaderboards and purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

- **Contract:** online features you choose to use, such as syncing, leaderboards and purchases.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the game working and improving it (crash reports and analytics, which you can turn off), and preventing fraud.
- **Consent:** personalized ads, and on iOS the App Tracking Transparency permission. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices** (shown where this applies).

## 4. Your ad and tracking choices

- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Gridwell shows Google's consent form before the first ad. Where required, the form also offers US state privacy choices, such as opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
- On iOS, Gridwell asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before the first ad. If you decline, ads are not personalized using the advertising identifier.
- You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings.
- Buying **Remove Ads** or **Premium** turns off the ads shown between puzzles. Premium removes all ads.

Our server is located in the European Union (Finland). Google and Apple may process data in other countries, including the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Data on your device | Until you delete it in Settings, or uninstall the app |
| Server account (anonymous ID, email address if you signed in, pen name, timezone offset, progress, daily times, achievements, settings) | Until you delete your data. Anonymous accounts that never played anything are removed automatically. |
| Purchase records and entitlements | Until you delete your data, or longer where tax or accounting law requires it |
| Notification tokens | Removed when they stop working, or after 90 days without use |
| Firebase Analytics | 14 months |
| Crashlytics crash reports | 90 days |
| Server application logs | 30 days. These record requests and errors, not your IP address. |

## 8. Deleting your data

- **In the app:** open **Settings > Privacy > Delete my data**. This deletes your server account and everything linked to it (progress, times, pen name, email address, achievements, notification tokens and the Firebase account), and clears the progress stored on your device. Your purchases belong to your Google Play or Apple account, so you can restore them afterwards.
- **Signing out** (Settings > Account) keeps your account; deleting your data removes it.
- **By email:** write to prantadutta1997@gmail.com and include your pen name, or the email address you signed in with. We will reply within 30 days.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. Children

Gridwell is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the version and date above and tell you in the app.
