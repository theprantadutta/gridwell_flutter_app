# Refund Policy for Gridwell: Nonogram Picross

**Policy Version: 1.0**  
**Effective Date: November 3, 2026**  
**Last Updated: November 3, 2026**

All purchases in Gridwell are made through **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. The store takes the payment, so refunds are issued by the store under its own policy. We are glad to help if something went wrong.

## What you can buy

- **Remove Ads:** a one-time purchase
- **Gridwell Premium:** a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, which may start with a free trial

## Requesting a refund

**Google Play (Android)**  
- Within 48 hours of purchase, you can usually request a refund yourself at https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory or through the Google Play Help Center.  
- After 48 hours, email us (below) with your order ID (it starts with `GPA.`) and we will review your request.

**Apple App Store (iOS)**  
- Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides on App Store refunds; we cannot issue them directly.

## Subscriptions and free trials

- Cancel any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or in your Apple ID subscription settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
- To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- We do not offer partial refunds for unused time in a subscription period, except where the law requires it or a technical problem stopped you from using Premium.

## If a purchase did not arrive

If you were charged but Remove Ads or Premium did not turn on, open **Settings > Restore purchase** first. If it still has not arrived, email us with your order ID and we will fix it or support a refund.

If a refund is granted, the related benefit (Remove Ads or Premium) is removed.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer laws of your country, such as the EU and UK right of withdrawal where it applies to digital content.
