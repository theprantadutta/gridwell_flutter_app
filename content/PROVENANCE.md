# Picture provenance

Every picture shipped in Gridwell must have clean, documented rights, because the app is sold
commercially on two stores.

## Policy

- `source: original` means the picture was drawn for Gridwell in this repo and is owned by the project.
- Third-party art is accepted **only** under CC0 (public domain dedication). It is recorded as
  `source: cc0:<short-ref>` in the picture file, with a row in the table below.
- Never: CC-BY-SA, any NC license, unlicensed art, art taken from other nonogram apps, or recognizable
  copyrighted characters, logos or trademarks.

## Third-party sources

None. Every picture in v1 is original.
